FAQ

Find quick answers to common questions

To upload your server configuration file, follow these steps:

  • Open the “Create New Scan” page.
  • Click the “Upload File” button in the Config File section.
  • Select your .conf or .nginx file.
  • The system will validate and upload your file securely.
  • Files must be under 5MB and in a supported format.

To reset your password, follow these steps:

  • Click “Forgot Password” on the login page.
  • Enter your email address.
  • Check your inbox for a reset link.
  • Follow the instructions to create a new password.
  • If no email arrives, check your spam folder.

To view your scan results, follow these steps:

  • Open the “Scan History” page from your dashboard.
  • Locate your completed scan.
  • Click “View Details” to see a full report.
  • Export your results as a PDF or CSV file if needed.
